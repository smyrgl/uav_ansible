# Flight data note, 2026-10-05: tuning flight and the mission that lost power

Two armed intervals on the first campaign morning. The tuning flight was flown
to completion and returned on RTL; the mission flight climbed normally and lost
all power at about 25 s (a ground fault in the power module after a reflow),
falling from the climb. Damage: one bracket and one landing leg. Separately,
the E1R LiDAR produced nothing all day: its 12 V came from the Castle BEC, which
gave out once the airframe was sealed. Everything below is from the bags on
`atomic` (`/srv/flights/flight_20261005_154849Z`, `..._155231Z`), their replays
and the FC's ulogs; no coordinates are recorded here.

## Stage 1 estimator truth (tuning flight, `tools/estimator_truth.py`)

Reference: 4295 RTK-fixed samples over 85.9 s of the 188 s armed interval (the
rest of the flight was not fixed), heading at 50 Hz, 84.6 % fixed-ambiguity,
1 cm horizontal accuracy. Extent 52 m.

| Estimator | APE, SE(3) aligned (rms / median / p95 / max) | APE, yaw-only aligned | Frame tilt | RPE 1 s | RPE 1 s yaw | Yaw drift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FAST-LIO `/lio/odometry` | 0.089 / 0.078 / 0.139 / 0.230 m | 1.308 m rms | 5.42° | 0.132 m rms | 0.55° rms, p95 1.28° | -0.7° per 5 min |
| PX4 `/px4/odometry` | 0.119 / 0.041 / 0.211 / 0.469 m | 0.122 m rms | 0.09° | 0.058 m rms | 0.53° rms, p95 1.23° | -0.2° per 5 min |

Gates (FAST-LIO): APE 0.089 < 0.5 m, RPE 0.132 < 0.15 m, yaw 0.55 < 1°: **TRUTH
PASS**. Two things the first run taught, both now in the scorer:

- FAST-LIO's 1.3 m yaw-only APE is entirely a 5.4° tilt of `camera_init`, its
  first IMU pose, which is as level as the Avia was at start. With the tilt
  removed the LIO is at 9 cm against RTK. The roadmap's gate is on the SE(3)
  error (`ape_se3`); the tilt is reported beside it and is the open item: a
  gravity-aligned `odom` out of the lio bridge (FAST-LIO estimates gravity in its
  state) so planners never see it.
- The first run failed yaw at 4.9° for both estimators identically. The scorer
  applied the compass-to-ENU conversion to `/gnss/atteuler`, which the gnss_ros
  driver already publishes as ENU yaw; that is a reflection, so it agreed in the
  hover and was off by twice every yaw change. With the convention right the
  receiver heading matches PX4's yaw at a 0.05° median offset and 0.40° rms over
  the whole flight, and FAST-LIO at 0.74° rms. The receiver's heading
  calibration is what it should be. The scorer also fits a heading stamp lag
  (found 10 ms, negligible) and can drop headings by the receiver's covariance.
- FAST-LIO's one divergence: a jump episode at 83 to 85 s of the matched
  interval (the end of the fixed window).

The mission bag scores SKIPPED: 1.7 m of reference motion, below the 2 m needed
to define an alignment.

## Replay fidelity (`uav-replay`, FAST-LIO replayed from the bag)

The replayed FAST-LIO trajectory diverges from the live one by 3.51 m median
(p95 6.27 m), map recall 1.5 % at 5 cm voxels: **REPLAY FAIL** on fidelity. Live
and replay are two runs of the same weakly constrained problem, an Avia alone
over an open field, from different initial states (the live run had been running
since boot). The live run itself is at 9 cm against RTK, so the fidelity gate
measures the determinism of the LIO on this terrain more than the pipeline; the
E1R back and a gravity-aligned, GNSS-anchored start are the levers.

## The FC's clock is GPS time, not UTC

The ulog-to-bag alignment (timesync, 188 samples) cross-checked against the
PPS captures gives an 18.007 s median residual on both flights, and the FC's
log names run 18 s ahead of the recorder's arming times. 18 s is the current
GPS-to-UTC leap-second count. PX4's Septentrio driver
(`src/drivers/gnss/septentrio/septentrio.cpp`, `time_utc_usec` from `wnc` and
`tow` through `mktime`) applies no leap seconds and sets the FC's clock from
that. Consequences: ulog UTC and log names 18 s ahead; the Remote ID
Location time mark 18 s ahead of true UTC. Fix in the FC branch: subtract the
`DeltaLS` of the SBF `ReceiverTime` block (or `GPSUtc`). The bag side is
unaffected: PVT and estimator stamps agree to within the 0.13 m APE, and a
±0.5 s shift costs 0.4 m.

## Pipeline fixes made on the day

- Offload/replay race: `flight.json` lands before the 16 GB MCAP, and the replay
  timer stamped the mission bag `.replayed` after a run that had nothing to
  replay. The offload now follows the data with a `.pushed` stamp and
  `uav-replay-new` requires it (deployed by hand to both hosts; the repo is the
  source).
- `uav-replay-new` logged `exited 0` for a failed step; exit codes are captured.
- The ulog fetcher linked the mission log to the tuning bag because the mission
  bag had no `flight.json` yet (tolerance 300 s); corrected by hand.
